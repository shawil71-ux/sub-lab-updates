SUB-LAB on the Brick (TrimUI's own system)
==========================================

Install / update
  1. Unzip SUB-LAB-trimui.zip. You get a folder called SUB-LAB.
  2. Copy that folder into the card's  Apps  folder (so you have Apps/SUB-LAB/launch.sh).
     From an Android phone: Brick > Settings > USB storage, connect a USB-C cable,
     then use the phone's Files app. Updating = copy the new folder over the old one.
  3. On the Brick: Apps > SUB-LAB.

Quit
  Yellow folder (top right) > Quit SUB-LAB.   Emergency exit: hold MENU + START for 2 seconds.

Where things go on the card
  Projects/   your projects          Presets/   synth presets
  Samples/    a good place for your sound library (any folder works)
  Recordings/ radio captures

If something's wrong
  sub-lab.log and launch.log (in this folder) say what happened. Send them to Claude.
  controls.txt (made on the first run) lets you change which button does what.

Not on the Brick yet: radio, recording from USB-C / the mic.
